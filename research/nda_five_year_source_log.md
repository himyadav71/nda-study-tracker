# NDA Five-Year Paper Corpus: Source Log

## Scope

This reanalysis treats the latest five calendar years available before the NDA II 2026 examination as **2022–2026 to date**: NDA I and II for 2022–2025, plus NDA I 2026. This creates a nine-paper corpus for each subject, or 1,080 Mathematics questions and 1,350 GAT questions at the standard paper sizes.

## Collection note

The direct Mathematics document was reached in-browser. Direct command-line retrieval stalled in this session, so remaining source capture will use browser-accessible locations or archived project material rather than treating the stalled transfer as evidence about paper content.

The official UPSC NDA I 2025 Mathematics URL listed in the earlier four-paper report was also checked on 17 August 2026. The UPSC edge service returned a processing error in this environment. The URL remains retained as primary-source provenance; this error does not affect the independently preserved 2024–2026 paper scans and labels already held in the project.

The CollegeDekho 2023 Mathematics tables list the same row values for NDA I and NDA II 2023 (for example, integration 9, sequence/series 8 and trigonometry 9 in both). The duplication is implausible as a question-level record, so these figures are retained only as a **low-confidence chapter-presence cross-check**, not as counts in the statistical aggregation.

The linked Google Drive archive for **NDA I 2022 Mathematics** opens successfully in the browser as a 48-page paper. It therefore becomes a direct document source for question-level classification, subject to preserving the original scan rather than relying on the page’s secondary topic table.

The linked Google Drive archive for **NDA I 2022 GAT** also opens successfully as a 48-page, 150-question paper. The two 2022 I scans have been downloaded from their public archive links and preserved locally for OCR-assisted question coding. Their source URLs remain the archive links in the table above; neither download is treated as an answer-key source.

The linked Google Drive archive for **NDA II 2022 Mathematics** opens successfully as a 48-page, 120-question paper. It will be retained as a direct historical source and processed with the same local OCR and fixed Mathematics taxonomy used for NDA I 2022.

The linked Google Drive archive for **NDA II 2022 GAT** opens successfully as a 48-page, 150-question paper. Both NDA II 2022 scans are now preserved locally and OCR’d for question-level classification; the public archive links remain the document provenance and are not used as answer-key sources.

The archive PDF for **NDA I 2023 Mathematics** opens directly in the browser as a 48-page, 120-question document at the NDA Study link recorded in the source table. This confirms an accessible historical document route, pending local extraction.

Exact archive document links were extracted from the NDA Study paper index for the remaining collection work: NDA I 2023 GAT (`NDA-NA-I-2023-GENERAL-ABILITY-TEST-by-NDA-Study.pdf`), NDA II 2023 Mathematics (`NDA-NA-II-2023-Maths-by-NDA-Study.pdf`), NDA II 2023 GAT (`NDA-NA-II-2023-GAT-by-NDA-Study.pdf`), NDA II 2025 Mathematics (`NDANA-II-2025-MATHEMATICS-by-NDA-Study.pdf`), and NDA II 2025 GAT (`NDANA-II-2025-GENERAL-ABILITY-TEST-By-NDA-Study.pdf`). These secondary archive copies are used only to recover question text; the report keeps an explicit source-quality caveat for all non-UPSC originals.

The NDA II 2025 Mathematics archive document is reachable, but its transfer rate became intermittent in the sandbox. A partially resumed download exists; the browser PDF viewer also exposed the document shell but did not finish rendering during observation. The final analysis therefore treats NDA II 2025 as a separately logged, lower-confidence supplemental source unless full local extraction completes.
